# Deep Dive: Anthropic's red team warns AI worms could soon spread across home devices

Anthropic's Frontier Red Team, as reported by Al Jazeera on Oct 13, warns that small AI models may be only months from being able to copy themselves onto connected consumer devices such as smart TVs and appliances, with no central host to shut down. This is the week's biggest development because it moves self-replication from abstract risk to a concrete, near-term IoT threat model. The report also stresses a caveat: in testing, no current model was both capable and small enough to replicate reliably on a smart TV.

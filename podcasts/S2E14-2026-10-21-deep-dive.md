# Deep Dive: Meta's Muse Spark Open — an 816B open-weight model built to run on a Mac

Meta released Muse Spark Open on Oct 15, an 816-billion-parameter, natively multimodal, 1M-token-context open-weight model aimed at coding and agentic work, which Meta calls the most capable open-weight model in the United States. This episode digs into what it takes to run it locally (TechCrunch reports roughly 1 TB of RAM, best on an M5 Ultra Mac Studio), the agentic demos Meta showed with its Muse Code terminal, and why it is the week's biggest development for the open-vs-closed frontier debate. Note: Meta's closed sibling Muse Wonder was not covered in this episode's sources. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/15/meta-launches-an-open-weight-model-that-runs-on-macs/

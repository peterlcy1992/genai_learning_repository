### Rufus-Air: An Open LLM Post-Training Recipe
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29421
- **Why it matters:** A fully open, reproducible post-training recipe on GLM-4.5-Air-Base (106B-A12B): eight serial stages (SFT → Reasoning RL → Coding RL → Instruction-Following RL → General Agent → Coding Agent → Search Agent → RLHF), moving from hard verifiable rewards toward softer judge-based signals. Built only from open components and public data — no new human annotation, no in-house distillation teacher — and it beats the official GLM-4.5-Air post-trained release. What's published here is the *recipe* (data, reward design, infra, stage order, stagewise results), which is rarer than published weights.
- **Relates to:** Stage 5 — Alignment & post-training.

### PoEM: Predicting RL Outcomes from Existing Policies
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30226
- **Why it matters:** Normally, changing or combining reward functions means rerunning RL post-training from scratch. PoEM instead mixes previously-trained single-reward policy adapters at inference time (a weighted log-mixture, no further training) to predict what RL on a new composite reward would have produced. Across 20 GRPO/DPO adapters on Qwen3-0.6B, it recovers most of the reward gain of a directly-trained composite policy — cheap iteration on reward design without a full RL run each time.
- **Relates to:** Stage 5 — Alignment & post-training.

### Your Transformer Can Hold Two Thoughts at Once: Evidence of Linear Superposition in LLMs
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29845
- **Why it matters:** Linearly combine two input text streams and a Transformer's next-token prediction turns out to be close to the superposition of what it would have predicted for each stream separately — evidence that, despite all the non-linear machinery, the model's outputs behave close to linearly. The effect is strongest early in pretraining and fades with more training, can be restored with light fine-tuning, and lets the authors decode two coherent continuations out of a single forward pass. A concrete probe into what the architecture you'll meet at Stage 1 is actually doing internally.
- **Relates to:** Stage 1 — The Transformer; also Stage 4 — Efficiency & building blocks.

## 📌 If you read one thing today
The linear superposition paper — it's the one most directly about the
Transformer architecture itself (Stage 1), rather than about the layer of
process built on top of it (post-training, RL). Seeing that a Transformer's
output behaves close to *linear* despite being built from non-linear parts
is a nice, concrete thing to have in the back of your mind once you get to
self-attention.

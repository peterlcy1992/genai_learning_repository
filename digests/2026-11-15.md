## Today's highlights

A moderate day. There is one fresh paper on how mixture-of-experts models are organized internally, and a preview of Alibaba's Qwen4-Max. Two items confirm releases we had flagged earlier as unverified: the Kimi K3.1 Pro open weights and Claude Fable 5.2.

### Understanding Mixture-of-Experts Language Models Beyond Expert Boundaries
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13975
- **Why it matters:** This paper looks inside MoE language models at the level of individual neurons rather than whole experts, and asks how specialization is actually organized. It also introduces a pruning method, RAMPr. The abstract says it keeps about 91–98% of full performance across six models while retaining 75% of neurons. That is useful evidence that MoE "experts" are not as cleanly separated as the name suggests. (Submitted 10 Nov 2026; via web search; page not fetched)
- **Relates to:** Stage 4 — efficiency / mixture-of-experts

### Alibaba opens a time-limited Qwen4-Max preview with 1M-token context
- **Type:** model release
- **Link:** https://alphasignal.ai/news/alibaba-launches-qwen4-max-with-agent-first-coding-and-1m-token-support
- **Why it matters:** Alibaba released `qwen4-max-preview` on Nov 11. It is aimed at agentic coding and long-running agent workflows, with a 1M-token context and image and video input. The preview is available only through Alibaba's subscription products and ends on Nov 25. No benchmark table, model card, parameter count or weights have been published, so this is a first look at Qwen4 rather than something you can evaluate yet. (via web search; page not fetched)
- **Relates to:** Stage 7 — agents & applications; Stage 8 — staying current

### Kimi K3.1 Pro weights now confirmed on Hugging Face (follow-up)
- **Type:** model release
- **Link:** https://technode.com/2026/11/05/moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-1-pro-model-weights/
- **Why it matters:** This follows up our Nov 4 entry, which flagged the release as "not confirmed." TechNode and other outlets now report that Moonshot published the K3.1 Pro weights on Hugging Face under a Kimi-specific license. Reports describe a 2.8T-parameter MoE with about 104B active parameters and a roughly 1M-token context. Moonshot also says it removed hacking-related training data before the open release. It is one of the largest open-weight models available. (via web search; page not fetched)

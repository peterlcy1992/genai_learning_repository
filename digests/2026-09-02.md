### OpenAI's Astra becomes the first model to cross the "Critical" cyber capability threshold
- **Type:** blog / safety framework update
- **Link:** https://openai.com/index/path-to-astra/
- **Why it matters:** Under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework, Astra is the first model assessed as reaching "Critical" cyber capability — finding and exploiting previously-unknown zero-days in hardened systems, and planning/executing end-to-end cyberattacks from only a high-level goal, without step-by-step human help. It will still ship, but with tightened access controls and monitoring — a real example of a capability threshold translating into deployment restrictions rather than staying theoretical.
- **Relates to:** Stage 5 — Alignment & post-training (capability evaluation, safeguards); also Stage 7 — Frontier systems.

### Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1
- **Type:** model release
- **Link:** https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1
- **Why it matters:** Same model, two safeguard tiers — Fable 5.1 generally available, Mythos 5.1 gated to trusted cybersecurity/life-sciences access.

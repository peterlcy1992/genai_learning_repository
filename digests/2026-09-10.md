### Stable Answers, Unfinished Reasoning: Why Self-Consensus Is Not a Safe Early-Exit Signal
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09989
- **Why it matters:** Tests 3,520 rules for stopping a reasoning model early once repeated probes agree on an answer ("self-consensus") — none pass preregistered safety gates on held-out benchmarks, because agreement shows an answer persists under probing, not that reasoning has actually finished. A caution for anyone chasing test-time-compute savings via self-consistency shortcuts.
- **Relates to:** Stage 7 — Frontier systems (reasoning models, test-time compute).

### Structural Process Supervision for Latent Chain-of-Thought Reasoning
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09928
- **Why it matters:** Latent reasoning (continuous embeddings instead of verbose CoT tokens) is cheaper but hard to supervise directly, which causes representation collapse. This paper aligns latent and explicit-CoT embeddings through a shared "prototype" space — a concrete recipe for supervising reasoning that's never turned into text.
- **Relates to:** Stage 7 — Frontier systems (reasoning models, CoT efficiency).

### Distribution-Consistent Inference for Dynamic Sparse Mixture-of-Experts
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09241
- **Why it matters:** Dynamic top-k expert routing at inference can cut MoE compute without retraining, but it shifts the token distribution away from what the router was trained on. This paper's correction keeps dynamic routing consistent with training — the second MoE-inference-efficiency paper in two days, alongside yesterday's training-free expert-halving trick.
- **Relates to:** Stage 4 — Efficiency & building blocks (MoE).

### VLX-VR: An Agentic-Aware Video Reasoning Model
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09985
- **Why it matters:** Instead of single-pass inference over a fixed video window, VLX-VR runs a Think–Memory–Observation loop — deciding what evidence it still needs, reading/writing memory, then continuing or answering — trained with RL over multimodal data and agent trajectories. State-of-the-art on the MINERVA benchmark, and a clean example of agentic iterative reasoning applied to video rather than just text/web tasks.
- **Relates to:** Stage 6 — Multimodality (video-language reasoning); also Stage 7 — Frontier systems (agents, tool/memory use).

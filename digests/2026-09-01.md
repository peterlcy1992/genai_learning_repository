### ContextPilot: Teaching Agents for Proactive Context Management via Fine-grained RL
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28476
- **Why it matters:** Tencent + Tsinghua (EMNLP 2026). Long-running agents accumulate an ever-growing working context as they retrieve and integrate information over many turns. ContextPilot trains agents with RL to actively plan, hold long-term memory, and "soft offload" stale context — beating baselines on long-context QA and deep-search tasks with a *smaller* context, not a bigger one.
- **Relates to:** Stage 7 — Frontier systems (agents, long context); also Stage 4 — Efficiency & building blocks.

### PLVR: Program Learning with Verifiable Rewards
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28421
- **Why it matters:** Rather than pushing reasoning further into model weights via more RL, PLVR moves verifiable intermediate steps into an explicit typed program and trains it with "symbolic backpropagation" through the program's structure. At matched compute, 30B models with PLVR beat standard RL-with-verifiable-rewards by 27.8 points (avg.) on LiveCodeBench v6 and Tau2Bench — a genuine alternative to "just do more RL" for checkable tasks.
- **Relates to:** Stage 5 — Alignment & post-training (RL post-training alternatives); also Stage 7 — reasoning models.

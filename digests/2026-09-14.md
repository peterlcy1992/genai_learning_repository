### NCP-ArchPreview: pretraining with Next Concept Prediction instead of next-token prediction
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10715
- **Why it matters:** Scales a latent-space language model to 8.9B parameters / 5.73T tokens (the largest such demonstration yet), adding a "Next Concept Prediction" objective — predicting discrete, multi-token concepts built from a product-quantized vocabulary of the model's own hidden states — on top of standard next-token prediction. It matches OLMo-3-7B's final pretraining loss using only 51% of the tokens and beats it by 2.45 points on downstream macro-average (+5.99 on GSM8K). A concrete sign that supervising above the token level can buy real training-efficiency gains.
- **Relates to:** Stage 1 — The Transformer (what next-token prediction is, and how a concept-level objective departs from it); also Stage 4 — Efficiency & building blocks.

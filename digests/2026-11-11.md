### Depth as a Candidate Source: Portfolio Allocation for Recurrent Test-Time Scaling in LLMs
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12293
- **Why it matters:** In recurrent-depth models, different numbers of loops reach different correct answers. That suggests spreading test-time compute across several depths rather than picking a single one. (via web search; page not fetched)
- **Relates to:** Stage 6 — reasoning & test-time compute

### Addition by Subtraction: Toward Mitigating Fragile Safety Alignment in LLMs
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06553v1
- **Why it matters:** Safety behavior tends to sit in a few sparse components, which is why jailbreaks and pruning can remove it. Spreading it out makes it sturdier, with up to ~90% lower attack success reported for some attacks. (via web search; page not fetched)
- **Relates to:** Stage 5 — alignment & post-training (safety)

### Feeling of Knowing in Large Language Models
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04834
- **Why it matters:** Asks whether a model can tell if it knows something after post-training has updated its knowledge. Explicit alignment improves these self-judgments, which ties into calibration and hallucination. (via web search; page not fetched)
- **Relates to:** Stage 5 — alignment & post-training

### Large Language Models as a Universal Searcher for Open-World Automated Machine Learning
- **Type:** paper
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12791
- **Why it matters:** An LLM agent tunes architecture, hyperparameters and data mix together. It reports a ~1B-parameter Diffusion Transformer reaching GenEval 0.91 after 896 GPU-days. An example of LLMs automating ML research itself. (via web search; page not fetched)

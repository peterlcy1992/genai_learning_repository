### AEF-1: first cross-lab minimum standard for independent AI evaluators
- **Type:** blog / policy
- **Link:** https://aievaluatorforum.org/initiatives/minimum-operating-conditions
- **Why it matters:** The AI Evaluator Forum published AEF-1, "Minimum Operating Conditions for Independent Third-Party AI Evaluations" — a voluntary baseline for the access, independence, and transparency evaluators need to meaningfully assess frontier models. xAI, OpenAI, and Anthropic all cosigned it within days of Dario Amodei's "We Must Pace the Frontier" essay (logged here yesterday), which had proposed exactly this step. ### Shanghai AI Laboratory ships Atria Dawn Preview, a 744B-parameter agentic MoE
- **Type:** model release
- **Link:** https://huggingface.co/internlm/Atria-Dawn-Preview
- **Why it matters:** An MIT-licensed, 744B-parameter mixture-of-experts model built on the GLM-5.2 base, with a 1M-token context window, trained via a "Verifiable Experience Pipeline": every training task is tied to a real execution environment where the model calls tools, produces artifacts, and only experience with externally-verified outcomes (tests, metrics, file state) is kept, rather than relying on preference judgments alone. A large, openly-licensed demonstration of environment-verified agentic training at scale.
- **Relates to:** Stage 4 — Efficiency & building blocks (MoE at scale); also Stage 7 — Frontier systems (agents, tool use, long context).
